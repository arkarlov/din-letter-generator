# DIN 5008 Standard for Business Letters

## 1. Anatomy of the Letter

A standard DIN 5008 business letter is divided into several distinct functional blocks. This modular anatomy ensures standardized processing, especially when using window envelopes.

- **Header (Briefkopf):** The topmost area reserved for the sender's branding, logo, and general company information.
- **Address Block (Anschriftfeld):** Positioned specifically to align with standard envelope windows. It contains a simplified sender return address and the recipient's full address.
- **Information Block (Informationsblock):** Located on the right side of the page, parallel to the address block. It contains specific communication details such as reference numbers, dates, and contact person details.
- **Body Text Area (Textfeld):** The main communication area. It houses the subject line, salutation, the main text body, closing formula, signature area, and an enclosure note.
- **Footer (Geschäftsangaben):** The bottommost section of the page, used for mandatory legal and commercial information (e.g., registration court, managing directors, bank details).

## 2. General Page Geometry

- **Page Size:** A4 (210 mm x 297 mm)
- **Left Margin:** 25 mm (Text body starting position)
  - _Note: The Address Block starts at 20 mm from the left edge._
- **Right Margin:** Minimum 10 mm (Standard often uses 20 mm for visual balance).
- **Hole Punch Mark (Lochmarke):** Located on the left edge at Y = 148.5 mm (exactly in the vertical middle of the page). Width: approx. 2 - 4 mm.

## 3. Form A (High Header / Hochgestellter Anschriftblock)

Form A allows for a smaller header area (27 mm) and is used when less space is needed for the company logo.

### 3.1. Coordinates & Dimensions (Form A)

- **Header Area:** Y = 0 mm to 27 mm.
- **Address Block:**
  - Starts at X = 20 mm, Y = 27 mm.
  - Total Height = 45 mm.
  - Width = 85 mm.
  - Contains _Zusatz- und Vermerkzone_ (Height: 17.7 mm, holds 3 lines).
  - Contains _Anschriftzone_ (Height: 27.3 mm, holds 6 lines).
- **Information Block:**
  - Starts at X = 125 mm.
  - Starts at **Y = 32 mm** (5 mm offset below the start of the address block).
  - Width = 75 mm (up to X = 200 mm).
- **Body Text:** Starts at Y = 85 mm (leaving at least 2 blank lines below the address/info blocks).
- **Fold Marks (Faltmarken):** Located on the left edge.
  - First fold mark: Y = 87 mm.
  - Second fold mark: Y = 192 mm.

## 4. Form B (Low Header / Tiefergestellter Anschriftblock)

Form B provides a larger header area (45 mm) and is widely used for modern corporate stationery.

### 4.1. Coordinates & Dimensions (Form B)

- **Header Area:** Y = 0 mm to 45 mm.
- **Address Block:**
  - Starts at X = 20 mm, Y = 45 mm.
  - Total Height = 45 mm.
  - Width = 85 mm.
  - Contains _Zusatz- und Vermerkzone_ (Height: 17.7 mm, holds 3 lines).
  - Contains _Anschriftzone_ (Height: 27.3 mm, holds 6 lines).
- **Information Block:**
  - Starts at X = 125 mm.
  - Starts at **Y = 50 mm** (5 mm offset below the start of the address block).
  - Width = 75 mm (up to X = 200 mm).
- **Body Text:** Starts at Y = 103 mm.
- **Fold Marks (Faltmarken):** Located on the left edge.
  - First fold mark: Y = 105 mm.
  - Second fold mark: Y = 210 mm.

## 5. Footer and Pagination

### 5.1. Footer (Geschäftsangaben)

- **Position:** Located at the bottom of the page.
- **Margins:**
  - Left: Aligns with the body text at X = 25 mm.
  - Bottom: Usually maintains a minimum distance of 10 mm from the bottom edge (Y = 287 mm).
- **Content:** Often arranged in 3 or 4 columns containing legal details, bank accounts, and commercial registry information.

### 5.2. Page Numbers (Paginierung)

If a letter has multiple pages, the pages must be numbered.

- **Position:** Bottom of the page, typically integrated into or positioned just below the footer area.
- **Format:** Usually "Page X of Y" (e.g., "Seite 2 von 3").
- **Alignment:** Centered or aligned to the right margin, often with a top margin isolating it from the footer text.
